## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one visual cipher, not an illustration. Keep only the minimum that identifies the subject: a contour, a pose, a direction. Let the form break, dissolve and stay incomplete so the viewer closes it. Use calligraphic or charcoal marks, a few dark strokes, limited color and clear negative space. The mark may be very small, off center or cropped. The ground is warm white with black-gray and one accent from the photograph. Type is a tiny art-publication note. Reject a complete drawing, a commercial icon, cartoon, over-geometry and smooth vector.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
